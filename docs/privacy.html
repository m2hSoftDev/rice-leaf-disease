1. Overview & Privacy Guarantee

Rice Leaf Disease Detection is an offline, privacy-first mobile tool designed for farmers, agricultural students, and researchers. The application operates entirely on your device without transmitting data over the internet.

  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, harvest, or store any personal information, device identifiers, or user photos on external servers.
  • Zero Tracking & Analytics: The application bundles no third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, advertising libraries, or crash reporting services.
  • Zero Network Traffic: The application does not require and does not request internet access permissions (android.permission.INTERNET is omitted).
  • On-Device Processing: Machine learning inference (MobileNetV2) runs locally on your device via TensorFlow Lite.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal data, including:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number).
  • Location information (neither precise nor approximate).
  • Device or network identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Android ID, IP address).
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking.
  • Financial or payment data.

3. Device Permissions and Usage

The application requests only runtime permissions required for local leaf analysis:

3.1 Camera (CAMERA)

Requested when the app opens, after onboarding, on the Scan screen, with a short rationale screen shown before the system permission prompt (Decision D-31). Photos are used only for on-device analysis and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared.

3.2 Photo Library (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Requested only when you choose to select a leaf photo from your device gallery. Only the selected image is read by the app.

4. Local Storage & User Control

Captured leaf photos and scan history (identified condition, confidence %, timestamp) are stored strictly within the private application sandbox on your device. You can permanently wipe this data at any time via the "Clear All" button in the History tab. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all application data.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

The application contains no third-party SDKs. Any outgoing links to open-source licenses open in your default browser subject to that website's policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The application does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
